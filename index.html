Tucked away in the heart of Warwickshire, The Granville Arms is a country pub full of character, good food and a genuinely warm welcome.

Our historic building has all the charm you would expect, with relaxed spaces to eat, drink and unwind. Pop in for a pint, catch up over dinner or settle in for Sunday lunch — there is always a place for you here.
